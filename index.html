HOW IT WORKS

    
A small model about whether you need a model.

    
It uses logistic regression with ten inputs from the questions on this page. Everything runs in your browser.

    
The first version was trained on 9,000 made-up project examples. The training rules are in the repository. So treat the number as a playful estimate, not a fact.

    
The useful part is the breakdown of what pushed the result up or down.
